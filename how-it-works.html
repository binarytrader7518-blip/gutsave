How Gut Save Works

The idea: support the gut from several directions

Your gut lining is a single layer of cells that decides what gets absorbed and what stays out. Your microbiome — the trillions of bacteria in your intestines — helps break down food and influences how your digestion feels day to day. When either is out of balance, bloating, gas and irregularity can follow.

Gut Save is formulated around both. The seller says the formula targets "hidden food toxins" that can irritate the gut lining and disrupt the microbiome, then helps rebalance things.† In practice, the ingredients fall into three groups:

1. Lining nutrients

Fish collagen peptides and colostrum. Both have been studied in relation to the intestinal barrier, mostly in lab and early clinical research.

Gut lining focus →

2. Microbiome support

Five Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium strains, plus lion's mane mushroom, which is being researched for its effects on gut bacteria.

Microbiome focus →

3. Traditional botanicals

Papaya seed and black cumin seed, both used for generations in traditional approaches to digestion.

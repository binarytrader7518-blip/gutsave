What is Gut Save?

Gut Save is a once-daily capsule supplement for people who deal with bloating, irregularity and that heavy, unsettled feeling after meals — and who are tired of quick fixes that don't last.

Rather than relying on a single probiotic, the formula pairs gut-lining nutrients (fish collagen peptides and colostrum) with organic lion's mane, papaya seed, black cumin seed extract and five live-culture strains. The seller describes it as a natural, non-GMO, stimulant-free blend.†

Supporting habits

A supplement works best alongside good habits

Gut Save is a dietary supplement in capsule form designed to support digestion and overall gut health.† It combines fish collagen peptides, colostrum, organic lion's mane, papaya seed, black cumin seed extract and five probiotic strains in a single daily capsule. Learn more on our product overview page.

What ingredients are in Gut Save?

The seller lists ten ingredients: Fish Collagen Peptides, Organic Lion's Mane Powder, Papaya Seed Powder, Black Cumin Seed Extract, Colostrum Powder, Lactobacillus paracasei, Lactobacillus buchneri, Lactobacillus brevis, Lactobacillus hilgardii and Bifidobacterium lactis BL-04. How does Gut Save work?

The seller positions Gut Save as a formula that supports the gut lining and helps rebalance the microbiome, aiming at the root of digestive discomfort rather than masking symptoms.† Collagen and colostrum relate to the gut lining, the five probiotic strains relate to gut bacteria, and the botanicals have a long history of traditional digestive use.

Gut Save FAQ

Clear answers about Gut Save ingredients, directions, pricing, shipping, the money-back guarantee and ordering.

What is Gut Save?

Gut Save is a dietary supplement in capsule form designed to support digestion and overall gut health.† It combines fish collagen peptides, colostrum, organic lion's mane, papaya seed, black cumin seed extract and five probiotic strains in a single daily capsule. Learn more on our product overview page.

What ingredients are in Gut Save?

The seller lists ten ingredients: Fish Collagen Peptides, Organic Lion's Mane Powder, Papaya Seed Powder, Black Cumin Seed Extract, Colostrum Powder, Lactobacillus paracasei, Lactobacillus buchneri, Lactobacillus brevis, Lactobacillus hilgardii and Bifidobacterium lactis BL-04. They are combined in a proprietary blend, so individual amounts are not published.

How does Gut Save work?

The seller positions Gut Save as a formula that supports the gut lining and helps rebalance the microbiome, aiming at the root of digestive discomfort rather than masking symptoms.† Collagen and colostrum relate to the gut lining, the five probiotic strains relate to gut bacteria, and the botanicals have a long history of traditional digestive use. See How It Works.

How do I take Gut Save?

Take one capsule each morning with a big glass of water, as directed by the seller. Do not exceed the label directions.

How long does one bottle last?

The bottle label shows 30 capsules. At one capsule a day, a bottle lasts about 30 days, so the 2-, 3- and 6-bottle packages cover roughly two, three and six months.

Yes. The seller offers a 60-day, 100% money-back guarantee. To receive a full refund, return all bottles (empty or not) along with the packing slip within the 60-day window.

Yes. US orders on the 3- and 6-bottle packages ship free and usually arrive in 5–7 working days. Canada, the UK and Ireland, and Australia and New Zealand ship for $15.95 and usually arrive in 10–15 working days.

Is this a one-time purchase or a subscription?

Each package is a one-time payment. The seller states there are no hidden charges and no automatic rebilling.

Does Gut Save contain stimulants or GMOs?

The seller describes Gut Save as a natural, easy-to-use formula with no stimulants and non-GMO ingredients.

Are there side effects or allergens to know about?

The seller does not publish side-effect information. Because Gut Save contains fish collagen and colostrum (from cow's milk), it may not suit people with fish or dairy allergies. Check the full label and talk with your doctor before use, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication or have a medical condition.

The seller does not publish a specific timeline, and individual results vary. Supplements tend to be evaluated over consistent daily use, which is one reason many buyers choose a multi-bottle package covered by the 60-day guarantee.
